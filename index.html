Interests

My research agenda is based on three pillars:

  • Decentralized Learning: to enable collaboration across users and sharing knowledge while preserving privacy and distributing computational efforts.
  • Continual Learning: to enable continuous knowledge integration allowing models to keep improving without forgetting previously acquired skills, unless on purpose.
  • Modular Learning: for creating models that are more reusable, efficient, and maintainable.
